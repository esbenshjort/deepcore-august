## 3. Eligibility rules

Eligible: Operating, Idle, CommutingHome, CommutingToWork  
Not eligible: Sleeping; NeedsCare; Injury ≥ 70  
Centralized in `SocialAuraEligibility.IsEligible`.

## 4. Distance / reach model

- EffectiveReach = Stage0 `Expression.Reach` × `ReachWorldScale` (2.35)
- Asymmetric: A may reach B without B reaching A
- Pair exposed if either reaches
- Falloff = (1 − d/maxReach)² inside reach; 0 outside
- No world aura circles for players (DEV gizmos optional)

## 5. Live InteractionPressure

- Gain ∝ gameHoursDelta × PressureGainPerGameHour × expressionWeight × falloff × contextMul × relationMul × focusDamp
- Decay while separated; strong overnight decay (`OvernightPressureDecayMul=3.2`)
- Cooldown after encounter (Stage 0 constant)
- Max 3 encounters / worker / shift
- No per-frame random rolls

## 6. Time basis

Uses same `hoursDelta = Time.deltaTime / SecondsPerGameHour` stamped in `TickClock`, consumed in `LateUpdate` after avatar sync — **game time, not FPS**.

## 7. Context derivation

- WorkingTogether: both Operating + same job OR conservative collab pairs (Prospect↔Refine, Engineer↔Excavate, Prospect↔Excavate)
- IdleNearby: default proximity
- SharedProblem / RecentSuccess / RecentFailure: short window on `WorkerStateEvent` history (both-side problem for SharedProblem)
- Does not inspect machine internals

## 8–9. Relationship / pair storage

- Directional Trust/Warmth/Hostility on `SocialAuraWorld` keyed by WorkerId (not provider)
- Pair transient pressure/cooldown/exposure runtime-only
- Relations persist across jobs/shifts/sleep

## 10. WorkerStateEvent integration

- Context **reads** event history

## 11. Camp / commute
